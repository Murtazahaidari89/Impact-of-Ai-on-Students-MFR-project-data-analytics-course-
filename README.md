Impact of AI on Students
MFR Project – Data Analytics Course
Project Overview

This project explores the impact of Artificial Intelligence (AI) on students and their academic lives. The objective is to analyze how students use AI tools, understand their perceptions of AI, and identify both the positive and negative effects of AI on learning, productivity, and academic performance.

Using data analytics techniques, this project examines survey data to uncover patterns, trends, and insights related to AI adoption among students.

Objectives
Analyze students' awareness and usage of AI technologies.
Identify the benefits students gain from using AI tools.
Explore the challenges and concerns associated with AI in education.
Examine the relationship between AI usage and academic performance.
Present findings through data visualizations and statistical analysis.
Provide recommendations for the responsible use of AI in educational environments.
Dataset

The dataset used in this project contains responses collected from students regarding their experiences and opinions about AI in education.

The data includes information such as:

Frequency of AI tool usage
Purpose of using AI tools
Perceived impact on learning outcomes
Student attitudes toward AI
Benefits and challenges of AI adoption
Demographic information (where applicable)

Conclusions and Recommendations
Key Findings

The analysis highlights several important insights:

Many students use AI tools to improve productivity and support learning activities.
AI can enhance understanding of complex topics when used appropriately.
Excessive reliance on AI may affect the development of independent critical thinking skills.
Students generally view AI positively but express concerns regarding academic integrity and ethical use.
Educational institutions should promote guidelines for the responsible use of AI technologies.

Conclusion

Artificial Intelligence is becoming an important part of modern education. While AI offers significant opportunities to improve learning efficiency and accessibility, it also presents challenges related to ethics, dependency, and academic integrity. This project provides data-driven insights that can help educators, institutions, and students make informed decisions about the use of AI in education.
